# AI/ML Conferences and Events

## Major AI/ML Conferences

### NeurIPS (Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems)
- **Website**: https://neurips.cc/
- **Focus**: Machine learning and computational neuroscience
- **CFP Period**: Typically February-March
- **Event Date**: December
- **Location**: Varies (North America/Europe)
- **Notes**: One of the most prestigious AI conferences

### ICML (International Conference on Machine Learning)
- **Website**: https://icml.cc/
- **Focus**: Machine learning research
- **CFP Period**: Typically January-February
- **Event Date**: July
- **Location**: Varies globally
- **Notes**: Top-tier ML conference with strong industry presence

### ACL (Association for Computational Linguistics)
- **Website**: https://www.aclweb.org/
- **Focus**: Natural language processing and computational linguistics
- **CFP Period**: Typically November-December
- **Event Date**: July
- **Location**: Varies globally
- **Notes**: Leading conference for NLP research

### CVPR (Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition)
- **Website**: https://cvpr.thecvf.com/
- **Focus**: Computer vision and pattern recognition
- **CFP Period**: Typically October-November
- **Event Date**: June
- **Location**: North America
- **Notes**: Premier computer vision conference

### ICLR (International Conference on Learning Representations)
- **Website**: https://iclr.cc/
- **Focus**: Deep learning and representation learning
- **CFP Period**: Typically September-October
- **Event Date**: May
- **Location**: Varies globally
- **Notes**: Open review process, strong industry participation
